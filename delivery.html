TRACENOTICEDELIVERY PROCESS
WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU START

A narrow intake. A concrete implementation deliverable.

TraceNotice is designed to reduce ambiguity. The work starts from a real public AI surface and ends with implementation instructions, acceptance tests and a reviewable evidence checklist.

01

Choose the scope

One public AI surface for the €190 Snapshot, up to three public client surfaces for the €490 Agency Pilot, or one coordinated deployment for the €2,500 Remediation Sprint.

02

Provide complete intake

Send the public URL or clearly identify the public flow, what the AI surface does, and any supported language or interaction path that materially changes the experience.

  • Public chatbot / assistant URL
  • Public voice-agent flow or number with safe test instructions
  • Public generative-content workflow
  • Relevant release or product context
03

TraceNotice records the observed state

The review captures what a user currently sees, hears or receives at the public surface and maps that state to the implementation question that needs to be resolved.

04

You receive implementation work—not a generic memo

The deliverable can include disclosure wording and placement, developer remediation instructions, acceptance criteria, marking/provenance verification tasks and an evidence checklist.

05

Preserve the evidence

The goal is a reviewable record: what was observed, what changed, how the change should be tested and what artifacts should be retained after deployment.

Turnaround targets

€190 Snapshot: target within 24 hours after complete intake. €490 Agency Pilot: target within two business days after complete intake. Complex remediation work is scoped separately.

Do not send secrets or confidential customer data through public channels.

Public-surface review should use public URLs and non-sensitive deployment context. Credentials, production secrets, private customer records and regulated personal data should not be posted to the public GitHub contact path.

SCOPE LIMIT

Technical implementation evidence, not legal certification.

TraceNotice does not certify legal compliance or replace qualified counsel. It makes the operational state concrete enough for product, engineering, procurement and counsel to review and act on.
