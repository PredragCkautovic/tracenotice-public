FAQ

Before you buy.

Is TraceNotice a law firm or certification service?

No. TraceNotice provides technical and operational implementation support around public AI transparency surfaces. It does not provide legal representation, issue certifications or guarantee legal compliance.

Why pay if free Article 50 checklists exist?

Because the paid output is tied to the actual deployed surface and is written as implementation work: observed state, exact change, acceptance criteria, evidence to retain and a remediation re-check. It is intentionally narrower than a broad governance program.

Do I need to share credentials or source code?

No for the standard public-surface offers. Public/non-confidential information is the default. Private work should be scoped separately before any confidential material is shared.

What happens after PayPal?

You return to the delivery page, download the implementation pack and submit the public surface(s) for review. TraceNotice counts revenue only after PayPal confirms payment.
