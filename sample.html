01 · OBSERVED STATE

The AI disclosure appears only after the user has already started the interaction.

Example observation: the launcher says “Ask Acme,” the pre-chat state does not identify the experience as AI, and “AI assistant” appears only in the conversation header after the first message.

02 · EXACT CHANGE

Move the disclosure into the pre-interaction state and keep a persistent AI label in-session.

SUGGESTED PRE-INTERACTION COPY
“Chat with Acme’s AI assistant.”
SUGGESTED FIRST-OPEN SUPPORT COPY
“You’re chatting with an AI assistant. For a person, choose Human support.”
